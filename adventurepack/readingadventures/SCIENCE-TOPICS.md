# Science Test Prep: scope and accuracy notes
Teacher's unit (3rd grade): motion, force, magnets, electromagnetism. A test is given at the end of the week; the app reviews and goes a little deeper.

## What the student should know
**Motion:** motion = change of position; position is described using a landmark; patterns (straight, back and forth, circle, up and down, zig-zag); speed (farther in the same time = faster); direction; patterns let us predict.
**Forces:** force = push or pull; forces start/stop/speed up/slow down/turn objects; bigger force = bigger change; balanced forces (equal, opposite) = no change in motion; unbalanced forces = motion changes; gravity pulls toward Earth; friction slows sliding, rougher surface = more friction; fair test = change one thing.
**Magnets:** poles N/S; opposites attract, likes repel; magnetic force works at a distance and through thin materials; closer = stronger; attracts iron/steel (nickel, cobalt) but NOT every metal (aluminum, copper are not magnetic); compass needle points north (Earth acts like a giant magnet).
**Electromagnetism:** electric current makes a magnetic force; electromagnet = coil of wire + iron core + battery; can be turned on/off; stronger with more coils/more current/iron core; flipping the battery swaps poles; uses: junkyard crane, doorbell, motor, speaker, maglev train.

## Notes for editors
- The labs are simple qualitative models (not exact physics): Force Lab distance = 6*push/(weight*(0.5+friction)); Electromagnet clips = round(coils*batteries/12) with an iron core (max 12).
- Ambiguous items were deliberately avoided (e.g. stainless-steel spoons, coins), since many everyday metal objects vary in magnetism.
