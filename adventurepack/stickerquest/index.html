Joseph Bruchac

Growing Up Near Mountains

Joseph Bruchac was born in 1942 and grew up in the mountains of New York. He lived in Greenfield Center with his grandmother and grandfather. Joseph loved to go everywhere with his grandfather. His grandfather showed him how to walk softly through the woods and taught him how to fish in the lakes and rivers.

As a child, Joseph worked in his grandparents' store. When he made mistakes, his grandfather talked with him about what happened, so Joseph learned how to do better the next time. In the winter, farmers would come to the store, sit around the stove, and tell stories about the mountains.

Joseph loved to read and write. His grandmother kept bookshelves filled with books, and he liked storybooks about animals.
